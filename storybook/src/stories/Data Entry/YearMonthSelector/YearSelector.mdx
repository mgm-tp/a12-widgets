The `YearSelector` widget is a standalone form field for picking a single year. It is also used
internally by `YearMonthSelector` and the date pickers.

- The `variant` prop selects the rendering mode: `select` (native dropdown), `textbox` (free input)
  or `autocomplete`. When omitted, the mode is auto-detected — `autocomplete` if a `yearRange` is
  provided, `textbox` otherwise.
- `yearRange` accepts absolute bounds (`{ start, end }`) or offsets relative to the reference year
  (`{ startOffset, endOffset }`); single-bound ranges are filled automatically.
- `optionalItem` adds a first entry that yields `undefined` when selected.
