The `LayoutGrid` widget is a responsive 12-unit grid composed of `LayoutGrid.Grid`,
`LayoutGrid.Row` and `LayoutGrid.Column`. Use it to arrange page or form content into
columns that adapt to the available space.

- The grid observes the width of its **own DOM element** (not the viewport) and switches
  between the `xs`/`sm`/`md`/`lg` breakpoints accordingly — resizing can be triggered by
  window resize or by CSS.
- Size each `Column` per breakpoint via `size={{ sm, md, lg }}`, or declare the whole row
  layout at once with `layoutConfig` on the `Row`.
- `noGutter`, `cellBorder`, `fitToParent` and `verticalAlignment` tune spacing, borders
  and alignment for the entire grid.
