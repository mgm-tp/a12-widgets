The `Wizard` widget guides users through a multi-screen flow as a horizontal sequence of steps.
It is a compound component: place `Wizard.Step` elements between an optional
`Wizard.PreviousStepButton` and `Wizard.NextStepButton`, and drive the flow yourself via each
step's `selected`, `finished` and `onClick` props.

- Steps support `finished`, `warning`, `error`, `disabled` and `nonInteractive` states plus an optional `icon`.
- Use `truncate` together with the step `title` prop when labels can get long.
- Use `responsive` (optionally tuned with `responsiveBehaviour`) to collapse steps that do not fit into a left-out (•••) step.

## Interaction hints

The `Interactions` sidebar entry shows the wizard wrapped in an `InteractionHintConfigProvider`
(`componentConfigs={{ wizard: true }}`), which replaces the browser's native `title` tooltips with
styled interaction hints on steps, navigation buttons and the responsive overflow menu.
