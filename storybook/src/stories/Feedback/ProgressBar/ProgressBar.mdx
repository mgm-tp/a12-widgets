The `ProgressBar` widget visualizes how far a determinate process has advanced, such as a download,
upload or installation. The fill width is driven by a single `percentage` prop (0–100), and changes
are animated with a smooth transition.

- The bar positions itself absolutely and stretches to fill its parent element — wrap it in a sized
  container (the parent automatically receives `position: relative`).
- If the parent has no background color, the bar renders its own themed track background; otherwise
  it inherits the parent's background.
