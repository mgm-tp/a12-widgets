The `ProgressIndicator` widget overlays a loading spinner on top of a content area to signal an
ongoing operation. Place it inside a `position: relative` container — it covers that container with
an outer overlay (blocking interaction) and centers a spinner in an inner overlay; use `global` to
cover the whole screen instead.

- An optional `label` is shown below the spinner (or beside it with `type="horizontal"`), and
  `useLoadingDots` appends animated dots to it.
- `outerOverlayVariant` / `innerOverlayVariant` (`bright` or `transparent`) control how strongly the
  underlying content is dimmed; `singleOverlay` collapses both into one.
- `focusOnOpen` and `scrollIntoView` help bring the indicator into view, and `openingDelay` /
  `fastAppear` tune when it becomes visible.
