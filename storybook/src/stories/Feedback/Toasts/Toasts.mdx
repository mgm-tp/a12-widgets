The `Toast` widget shows a short, non-blocking notification about the outcome of an action or a
system event. Each toast has a `variant` (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `error`) that controls its
color and icon, an optional `header`, a `message` and an optional `footer` for action buttons.

- A `temporary` toast (the default) invokes `onClose` automatically after `duration` milliseconds;
  a `permanent` toast stays visible until the user closes it.
- Wrap toasts in a `ToastGroup` to stack them at a screen corner (`position`), animate them in and
  out, and get focus handling plus Esc-to-close for free.
