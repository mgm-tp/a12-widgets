The `ModalNotification` widget displays a notification inside a modal overlay that blocks the rest
of the page until it is dismissed. Use it for messages that require the user's immediate attention
or an explicit decision before continuing.

- The `variant` prop (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `error`) controls the color scheme and default icon; a custom `icon` can be supplied instead.
- Dismissal is opt-in via `onClose` combined with `enableCloseButton`, `closeOnEsc` and/or `closeOnOutsideClick`.
- `footer` and `headingButtons` add action areas below the content and in the header.
- Focus moves into the modal on open and back to the trigger on close (`focusOnOpen`, `focusBack`).
