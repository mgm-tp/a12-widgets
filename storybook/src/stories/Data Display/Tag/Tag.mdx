The `Tag` widget renders a compact label, typically used to categorize or mark content with
keywords such as statuses, topics or filter criteria. Tags are often displayed in groups.

- Use `removable` to show a remove (×) button that fires `onRemove`; combine it with
  `disabledRemoveButton` to keep the button visible but inactive.
- Use `icon` to prepend an icon and `color` to override the background color.
- Tags are keyboard-reachable and support `onClick` and `onKeyDown` handlers.
