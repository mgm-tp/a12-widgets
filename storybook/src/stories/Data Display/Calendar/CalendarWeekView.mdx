The `Calendar` widget with `view="week"` displays a single week as columns of days, giving each
day's items more vertical space than the month grid. Use it when users need to see the details of
events within one week — the larger day cells suit richer item content rendered via
`componentRenderers.dayItemContentRenderer`.

- Day and item selection are controlled via `selectedDate`/`onDayClick` and `selectedItem`/`onItemClick`.
- The day header labels can be customized with `weekDayFormatter` and `dateFormatter`.
- Weekends and `publicHolidays` can be highlighted, and individual days can be disabled with `isDisabledDay`.
