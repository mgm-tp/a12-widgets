The `Calendar` widget with `view="month"` displays a full month as a grid of days, where each day
can carry data items (events) supplied via `calendarDateItems`. Use it to give users an overview of
scheduled events, deadlines or appointments for a whole month.

- Day and item selection are controlled via `selectedDate`/`onDayClick` and `selectedItem`/`onItemClick`.
- Item rendering is fully customizable through `componentRenderers` (e.g. `dayItemContentRenderer`).
- Weekends and `publicHolidays` can be highlighted, and individual days can be disabled with `isDisabledDay`.
- Days belonging to adjacent months are controlled with `outsideDayDisplay` (`none`, `disabled` or `active`).
