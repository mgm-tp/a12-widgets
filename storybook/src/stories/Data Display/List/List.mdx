The `List` widget renders a vertical collection of items using the compound components
`List.Item` and `List.SubHeader`. Each item can combine a `graphic` (e.g. an icon), primary
`text`, `secondaryText` and a trailing `meta` element.

- Items become interactive when given an `onClick` handler and support `selected`, `active`, `disabled` and `readonly` states.
- `border` frames the whole list; `divider` adds separators below items (per-item also as `"light"` or `"dark"`).
- `flipped` swaps the position of `text` and `secondaryText`; `paddedLeft`/`paddedRight` align items with and without graphics or meta elements.
