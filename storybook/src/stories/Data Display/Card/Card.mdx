The `Card` widget is a surface container that groups related content and actions about a single
subject. Compose it from its section subcomponents:

- `Card.Content` — the textual body of the card.
- `Card.Media` — a dedicated area for images or videos, usually placed at the top.
- `Card.ActionArea` — wraps sections to make the whole card clickable; it is keyboard focusable,
  fires `onClick` on Enter and shows hover/focus effects. The effect is suppressed automatically
  while hovering nested interactive elements.

Cards stretch to the full height of their container, which keeps them evenly sized in grid layouts.
