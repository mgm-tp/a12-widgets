The `Portal` widget transports its children into a React portal appended to `document.body` (or the
`WidgetsRoot` element), outside the current DOM tree — the foundation for modals, dropdowns and
other overlays. It handles dismissal itself: `closeOnEsc` and `closeOnOutsideClick` trigger the
`onClose` callback.

`AttachedPortal` builds on the same idea but positions its content relative to a `referenceElement`,
choosing an `orientation` (e.g. `bottom-start`, `top-end`) so the content stays fully visible inside
the viewport. It also offers fine-grained control via `orientationList`, `fixedOrientation`,
`focusOnOpen` and focus restoration after closing.
